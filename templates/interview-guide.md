# Interview guide — <learning goal>

## Before the call
- Consent: ask permission to take notes / record; say how notes are kept (private workspace).
  Legal questions about consent or recording → lawyer member.
- Pseudonym code for this person: C-nn

## Warm-up (2 min)
- Their role and context (team size, tools they use today).

## Story questions (25 min) — past behaviour, not opinions
1. Tell me about the last time you <did the job>. What happened?
2. What did you do next? Who else was involved?
3. What was hardest about it? What did it cost you (time, money, stress)?
4. What have you tried to fix it? Why did / didn't that work?
5. How often does this happen?

## Avoid
- Pitching the idea · "Would you use / buy…?" · leading questions · talking more than listening.

## Close (3 min)
- Is there anything I should have asked? Who else should I talk to?
- Commitment signal (next step they offer: a follow-up, an intro, a pilot) — note it.

## Notes template
- Context · Story · Pains & workarounds · Cost · Verbatim quotes · Commitment signals
