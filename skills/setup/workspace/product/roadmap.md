## Prioritisation (RICE)

> RICE = Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort. Reach per quarter from data (say the source) or a
> labelled estimate; Impact 3 / 2 / 1 / 0.5 / 0.25; Confidence 100% / 80% / 50% (below 50% → do
> discovery first); Effort in person-months from the `cto` member or the founder.
