# Feedback log

> Every piece of customer or user feedback worth keeping: interviews, support threads, sales
> calls, reviews, survey answers, the founder's own observations. Written by `feedback-log` and
> `discovery`. Never delete a row; change status. People are pseudonymised (segment + a code such
> as `C-07`); the mapping to real names stays with the founder or in `discovery/notes/`.
>
> - **Source:** interview · support · sales · survey · review · usage-data · founder · other member (e.g. `sales:…`)
> - **Type:** problem · request · praise · bug · question · churn-reason
> - **Opportunity:** the node in `discovery/OST-<product>.md` it supports (or `—`)
> - **Status:** new · triaged · linked · closed (with reason)
